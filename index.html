Hybrid Plan v2

3 Gym Days + Daily Grease-the-Groove

Overview & Context

Context: Fat loss is now secondary. Primary goal is protecting time for kernel-dev prep.

Structure: 3× ~2hr gym sessions (Mon/Wed/Sat) covering all major lifts once each, one explosive/power exposure per week, and daily <30min maintenance work on off days so nothing decays.

Monday — Squat + Power + Pull Gym Day (2 hrs)

Exercise Sets × Reps Notes
Warm-up 10 min Dynamic — leg swings, bodyweight squats, band work
Power Snatch 5 × 3 Technique/power priority, not load priority. Start with empty bar/light plates until the pull, turnover, and catch are clean and repeatable. Do this first, while fresh.
Back Squat 4 × 3–6 RPE 7–8
Romanian Deadlift 3 × 6–8
Weighted Pull-ups 4 × 4–8 Progress toward 5 × 3–5 weighted
Hanging Leg Raise 3 × 8–15
Calf Raise 3 × 10–15 Only if time remains
Mobility 5–10 min Ankle dorsiflexion, 90/90 hips, couch stretch

Tuesday — Grease the Groove Micro-Session (≤30 min)

Pick a fixed sequence and run it on a timer — the point is zero decision-making. No warm-up fluff.

Focus Details Notes
Jump Rope 5 min Steady pace
Sprints 4–6 × 20–30 m Or jump rope intervals if no space
Pull-ups 3–5 sets Submax. Spread through the day or in one block
Mobility 5 min Optional stretch/mobility

Wednesday — Bench/Push + Pull Balance Gym Day (2 hrs)

Exercise Sets × Reps Notes
Warm-up 10 min Shoulder/lat mobility
Bench Press 4 × 3–6 RPE 7–8
Overhead Press 3 × 5–8
Weighted Dips 3 × 6–10
Barbell/Cable Row 3 × 6–10
Ab Wheel 3 × 6–12
Handstand Practice 5–10 min Technique only, not conditioning
Mobility 5–10 min Shoulder + thoracic

Saturday — Deadlift + Athletic Power + Calisthenics Gym Day (2 hrs)

Exercise Sets × Reps Notes
Warm-up 10 min Sprint-style — A-skips, leg swings, easy jog
Deadlift 3 × 3–5 RPE 7–8
Broad Jump / Box Jump 3 × 3 each Max explosiveness, complements the snatch
Front Squat / Bulgarian Split Squat 3 × 5–8 (or 8-10/leg)
Pull-ups 3 × submax Leave ~2 reps in reserve
Push-ups 3 × 10–20
L-sit 3–4 sets
Mobility 15–20 min Saturday has the most slack — bank pancake, hip flexors, hamstrings, deep squat, ankle, shoulder, thoracic here
