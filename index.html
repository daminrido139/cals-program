Why It’s Laid Out This Way

  • Frequency: Each pattern is hit twice a week: push Mon/Sat, pull Wed/Sat, legs Mon/Wed.
  • Leg Spacing: Mon and Wed legs are only 48 hours apart, so they are split into squat-focused versus hinge-focused to avoid overlap.
  • Sprint Placement: Sprints sit on Saturday, which has no leg lifting, and are 3 days after your deadlifts and 2 days before squats.
  • Sprint Priority: Do sprints first while fresh. If your hamstrings feel tight, cut sprint intensity before you cut anything else.
  • Time Management: Each session is about 75-90 minutes with rests of 2-3 min on main lifts and 90 sec on the rest.

What was dropped: Accessories, abs, arms, lateral raises, and the power snatch. Note: If you want the snatch back, put it first on Monday and drop the Bulgarian split squats.

To Make Fat Loss Work on 3 Days

  • Nutrition: Keep a moderate deficit (about 300-500 kcal) and protein around 1.6-2 g/kg.
  • NEAT: Walk 8-10k steps on off days. It’s the cheapest replacement for the cardio you removed.
  • Intensity: Push every main set close to RPE 8, since you have fewer sets to work with.
  • Progression: Add weight or reps whenever you hit the top of a rep range.
