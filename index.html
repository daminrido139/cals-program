Hybrid Training Program

Strong, Lean, Fast, Mobile, Muscular & Capable

Weekly Schedule Overview

This structure gives you 2 sprint + 3 powerlifting-focused + 4 calisthenics exposures, without trying to make every day maximally hard.

Day Main Focus
Monday 🏋️ Squat + Lower strength
Tuesday 🏃 Sprint + Pull calisthenics
Wednesday 🏋️ Bench + Push calisthenics
Thursday 🏋️ Deadlift + Lower strength
Friday 💪 Upper strength + Calisthenics
Saturday 🏃 Sprint + Athletic Calisthenics + Mobility
Sunday 😴 Rest

Why this works: Heavy squat (Mon) → Sprint (Tue) → Push (Wed) → Deadlift (Thu) → Upper (Fri) → Sprint (Sat). This gives your legs reasonable recovery between the two high-intensity sprint sessions.

Progression System & Hierarchy

1. The Lifts (Linear & Conservative)

Don't chase maximum weights frequently. Start conservatively. When you can complete all sets at the top of the rep range with good technique, add a small amount of weight.

  • Squat & Bench: 4 × 3–6
  • Deadlift: 3 × 3–5

Example Squat Progression:

  • Week 1: 60 × 4 × 4
  • Week 2: 60 × 5 × 4
  • Week 3: 60 × 6 × 4
  • Week 4: 62.5 × 4 × 4

2. Calisthenics Skill Hierarchy

You don't need to do every advanced movement simultaneously. Master the foundation, then level up.

  • Pull: Pull-up → 10+ clean pull-ups → Weighted pull-up → Muscle-up → Front lever
  • Push: Push-up → Dips → Weighted dips → Pike push-up → Handstand push-up
  • Core: Hanging knee raise → Hanging leg raise → L-sit → Advanced L-sit / compression → Front lever progression
